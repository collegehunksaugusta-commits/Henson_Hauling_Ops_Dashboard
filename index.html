Extra Labor ▼

$52.58/hr/person regular · $74.86/hr/person overtime. Only for services not otherwise covered elsewhere in this tariff.

Valuation Coverage ▼

Option 1 — Released Value Protection (no charge, $0.60/lb per article)
Option 2(a) — Full Value, No Deductible ($15.73 per $1,000 declared value)
Option 2(b) — Full Value, $300 Deductible ($5.17 per $1,000 declared value)
Suggested value = higher of (a) weight × $6.00/lb — the general moving-industry standard for estimating Full Value Protection, not part of Tariff No. 7 itself — or (b) $5,000 × rooms, the minimum GA DPS allows a carrier to require. If the customer knows their goods are worth more than either figure, declare the real value instead.

Storage in Transit ▼

$8.40/100 lbs for the first day, then $0.25/100 lbs/day after. If Full Value valuation is selected above, an additional 10% of the storage charge applies.
