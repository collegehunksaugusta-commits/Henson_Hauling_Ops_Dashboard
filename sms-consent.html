Who receives these messages

We send work-related text messages only to our own employees — never to customers or the public, and never for marketing. Messages include schedule and job updates, safety and weather alerts, and company announcements, sent by our office managers.

How employees opt in

Every employee uses our employee app (the HUNK Portal). The first time an employee signs in after texting is turned on, the app shows the screen below. The employee enters or confirms their mobile number and checks the box to agree. Employees who choose No thanks are not texted, and agreeing is not a condition of employment. We record each employee’s answer, the date and time, and the exact wording they agreed to.
